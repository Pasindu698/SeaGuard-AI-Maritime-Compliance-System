TITLE: SOLAS Chapter II-2 - Fire Protection, Fire Detection and Fire Extinction

SOURCE ORGANIZATION: International Maritime Organization (IMO)

SOURCE:
International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea (SOLAS), 1974

REFERENCE:
SOLAS Chapter II-2

CATEGORY:
Fire Safety

SUMMARY:

SOLAS Chapter II-2 contains requirements concerning fire protection,
fire detection and fire extinction on ships.

The chapter follows a fire safety approach covering prevention,
detection, containment, fire-fighting and safe escape.

KEY REQUIREMENTS AND AREAS:

1. Prevention of ignition of combustible materials and flammable liquids.

2. Limitation of the growth potential of a fire within ship spaces.

3. Reduction of hazards caused by smoke and toxic products generated
during a fire.

4. Detection of fire in the space where it originates and provision
of alarms to support safe escape and fire-fighting activities.

5. Control of the spread of smoke.

6. Containment of fire within the space of origin.

7. Suppression and extinguishing of fire.

8. Protection of the structural integrity of the ship against
heat-related deterioration.

9. Notification of crew and passengers when a fire occurs.

10. Provision of suitable means of escape.

11. Maintenance and monitoring of fire safety systems and equipment.
