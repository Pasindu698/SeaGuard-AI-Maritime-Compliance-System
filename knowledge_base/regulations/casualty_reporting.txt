TITLE: Marine Casualty Investigation and Reporting

SOURCE ORGANIZATION: International Maritime Organization (IMO)

REFERENCES:

SOLAS Regulation I/21
SOLAS Regulation XI-1/6
Resolution MSC.255(84) - Casualty Investigation Code

CATEGORY:
Casualty Investigation and Reporting

SUMMARY:

IMO instruments establish requirements relating to the investigation
and reporting of marine casualties and marine incidents.

SOLAS Regulation I/21 addresses casualty investigations and the
provision of relevant information concerning investigation findings.

SOLAS Regulation XI-1/6 provides additional requirements concerning
the investigation of marine casualties and incidents and links those
investigations with the Casualty Investigation Code.

The Casualty Investigation Code was adopted through IMO Resolution
MSC.255(84).

IMPORTANT AREAS:

1. Marine casualties and incidents may require safety investigation.

2. Relevant findings from investigations are reported in accordance
with applicable IMO requirements.

3. The Casualty Investigation Code establishes international standards
and recommended practices for marine safety investigations.

4. Investigation information can support identification of contributing
factors and prevention of similar casualties.

5. Reporting of marine casualties and incidents to IMO is carried out
through the applicable reporting framework.
