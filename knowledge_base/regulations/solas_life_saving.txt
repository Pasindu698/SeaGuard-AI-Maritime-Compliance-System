TITLE: SOLAS Chapter III - Life-Saving Appliances and Arrangements

SOURCE ORGANIZATION: International Maritime Organization (IMO)

SOURCE:
International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea (SOLAS), 1974

REFERENCE:
SOLAS Chapter III
International Life-Saving Appliance (LSA) Code

CATEGORY:
Life-Saving and Rescue

SUMMARY:

SOLAS Chapter III establishes requirements for life-saving appliances
and arrangements carried on ships.

The International Life-Saving Appliance (LSA) Code provides detailed
technical requirements for life-saving appliances.

IMPORTANT AREAS:

1. Communications used during emergencies.

2. Personal life-saving appliances.

3. Muster lists and emergency instructions.

4. Operating instructions for life-saving equipment.

5. Survival craft muster and embarkation arrangements.

6. Stowage of survival craft.

7. Stowage of rescue boats.

8. Launching and recovery arrangements.

9. Recovery of persons from the water.

10. Emergency training and drills.

11. Operational readiness, maintenance and inspection of life-saving
equipment.

EXAMPLES OF LIFE-SAVING APPLIANCES:

- Lifeboats
- Liferafts
- Rescue boats
- Lifebuoys
- Lifejackets
- Immersion suits
- Visual distress signals
- Marine evacuation systems
- General alarm systems
