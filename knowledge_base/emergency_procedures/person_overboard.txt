TITLE: Person Overboard Emergency Response

CATEGORY:
Emergency Procedure

INCIDENT TYPE:
person_overboard

PURPOSE:

Provide a structured response sequence for a person-overboard emergency
for use by the SeaGuard AI prototype.

RESPONSE ACTIONS:

1. Raise the person-overboard alarm and immediately alert the bridge.

2. Maintain visual contact with the person when possible.

3. Follow the vessel's person-overboard manoeuvring and emergency
procedures.

4. Prepare appropriate life-saving and recovery equipment.

5. Follow the vessel's procedures for deploying rescue equipment
and rescue personnel.

6. Recover the person using the vessel's approved recovery arrangements.

7. Provide appropriate first-aid or medical assistance following
recovery when required.

8. Account for personnel and communicate the outcome to the bridge.

9. Record relevant information about the incident and response.

RELATED KNOWLEDGE:

SOLAS Chapter III
International Life-Saving Appliance Code
Ship Safety Management System
