TITLE: Shipboard Collision Emergency Response

CATEGORY:
Emergency Procedure

INCIDENT TYPE:
collision

PURPOSE:

Provide a structured response sequence for a collision emergency
for use by the SeaGuard AI prototype.

RESPONSE ACTIONS:

1. Raise the appropriate emergency alarm.

2. Notify the bridge and responsible officers.

3. Determine whether any person has been injured or is missing.

4. Assess the location and apparent extent of vessel damage.

5. Check affected areas for water ingress where safe to do so.

6. Monitor the vessel's condition and stability.

7. Identify any immediate fire, pollution or other secondary hazards.

8. Follow the vessel's emergency and damage-control procedures.

9. Maintain communication between relevant emergency response teams
and the bridge.

10. Record relevant information about the collision and actions taken.
