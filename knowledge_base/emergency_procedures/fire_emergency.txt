TITLE: Shipboard Fire Emergency Response

CATEGORY:
Emergency Procedure

INCIDENT TYPE:
fire

PURPOSE:

Provide a structured response sequence for a shipboard fire emergency
for use by the SeaGuard AI prototype.

RESPONSE ACTIONS:

1. Raise the emergency alarm immediately.

2. Notify the bridge or responsible officer.

3. Identify the location and nature of the fire when it is safe to do so.

4. Warn personnel in the affected area.

5. Follow the vessel's fire emergency plan and muster instructions.

6. Isolate the affected area where appropriate and safe.

7. Use appropriate fire-fighting equipment in accordance with the
ship's procedures.

8. Maintain safe escape routes for personnel.

9. Account for crew and other persons according to the vessel's
emergency procedures.

10. Monitor the situation and communicate changes to the bridge or
responsible officer.

11. Record relevant information about the incident and response actions.

RELATED KNOWLEDGE:

SOLAS Chapter II-2
Ship Safety Management System
Shipboard Emergency Plan
